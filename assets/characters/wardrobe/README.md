# Separated original wardrobe

Each item has a GLB, a Godot wrapper under scenes/characters/wardrobe/, and a
standalone editable Blender scene under source/. Their original materials,
packed textures, skin weights, rest fitting and skeleton are preserved.

| Asset | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| field_jacket | Jacket shell, sleeves, pockets, cuffs and hardware |
| collar_and_underlayer | Collar, lapels and visible underlayer |
| cargo_trousers | Trousers and cargo pockets |
| boots | Boots, soles, laces and hardware |
| gloves | Both articulated gloves |
| belt_and_strap | Belt, buckle and shoulder strap |
| shoulder_bag | Satchel, flap and closures |
| flashlight | Torch housing, grip, lens and switch |

These fit the previous dressed character. They have deliberately not been
refitted to the anatomy mannequin yet. Review and refine body proportions
first; then fit garments to the agreed body, one garment at a time.

The dressed character source and gameplay scene are preserved. No MakeHuman
skin, face or hair assets are included in these wardrobe exports; the clothing
geometry and materials were authored for Ashfall.
