Shared objects should recur naturally. A desk can be reused in several buildings; the paperwork, wear, equipment, arrangement and human traces establish its role. Do not give every building the same rubble, barrels and cot simply because those assets exist.

## Ordered work

1. **Existing-art audit and source shortlist — first pass complete.** Local image and asset index, licensing sources, format screening, duplicate rejection, unresolved gaps. No new images generated.
2. **Reference library by building — underway.** Named domestic, laboratory and relay equipment sources now recorded; bathroom fixtures, ordinary beds/tables and location-specific practical references remain open. Record origin and permitted use. Resolve the region/era from existing art plus user preference before imposing a real-world cultural setting.
3. **Reuse selections.** For each missing object, choose an existing local asset, a named external candidate, an adaptation, or an original design brief. Mark external files as listed / acquired / inspected separately. Spend only after a concrete purchase decision.
4. **Crossroads concept package — started.** Existing construction renders plus new dusk exterior and interior studies are available. Yard/storytelling, annotated layout and material/detail board remain. Reuse its existing silhouette and equipment. Produce additional views only where these references do not answer the question. Generated concepts are visual proposals, not measured construction plans.
5. **Building family packages.** Cottage households, checkpoint, research and relay each receive matching exterior/interior concepts, a prop board, material choices and story details. Establish what differs before requesting more variants.
6. **Items and weapons.** Audit the eight existing item appearances against their hand poses and inventory icons. Create variant studies only for meaningful gaps. Evaluate sourced pistol/rifle/shotgun art before original weapon design. Concept carrying and wear without implementing combat.
7. **Characters, creatures and anomalies.** Reuse suitable anatomy and clothing foundations; research distinctive movement and sensory cues. Develop Ashfall-specific silhouettes and traces in the environment. Avoid copying reference-game monsters or relying on a generic zombie pack for identity.
8. **Art approval and later production handoff.** Approved views, scale intentions, material sheets, source/credit records and a reuse-versus-create list. Only then resume 3D modifications, imports and gameplay integration.

## Per-asset record

Keep: stable ID; building/function; source link or local path; author; license evidence; cost if confirmed; native editable source; exchange format; textures; dimensions; rig/animation status; duplicate overlap; intended changes; missing evidence; decision; approval status.

## Visual direction to test

Start from the project's worn plaster/brick, faded painted timber, corrugated roofing and practical steel equipment. Test clear overcast daylight for construction readability, cold dusk for navigation and limited amber work lights for occupied-space traces. Concentrate deterioration around leaks, handling, traffic and repairs. Keep evidence of ordinary life and useful maintained objects among the decay.

This is a proposed direction derived from the existing art, not a final approved world bible. Threat visibility, weapon use, damage balance and new game systems are outside this art pass.
