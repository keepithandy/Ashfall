# Playable mannequin credits

The mannequin derives from the existing ../male_mannequin/ anatomy asset,
adapted from MakeHuman's CC0 core graphical body and adult male target.
See ../male_mannequin/CREDITS.md and the preserved CC0 asset license at

The flashlight, materials and grip detail were authored for Ashfall and reused
from the separated wardrobe. The player derivative adds the item socket,
per-model grip calibration, revised palm weights and draft locomotion clips.
