## Shared world rules

Working assumption: a fictional temperate rural district with ageing civilian industry and a later field-research presence. The exact country and year remain open. Keep manufacturers, uniforms, language and dated insignia out of early boards until that decision is made. An older appliance can survive into a later period, but a collection of antiques alone does not establish a coherent setting.

Use the existing plaster, brick, faded timber and practical steel palette. Buildings were useful before the horror: equipment follows work, dirt follows handling, water follows gravity. Preserve clear circulation, usable door swings and the existing paths around Crossroads. Concept art must show both a readable everyday view and the intended threatening lighting; darkness cannot conceal unresolved construction.

Common furniture may repeat. A cupboard becomes domestic through dishes and folded fabric; storage in research uses numbered containers and separated supplies. Give each place one strong object cluster and a few personal traces, leaving ordinary empty surfaces. Props should not imply pickups or working controls unless gameplay already supports them.

## B01 — Crossroads: repair work interrupted

**Keep:** the refined 12 × 8 m station and source blend; shallow roof, gutter, window/shutter/personnel-door relationship, ramp, damaged wall and yard. The existing bench, vise, toolbox, shelving, jack, trestle, generator, tyre, pallet, pipe stock and hose cover most needs. Read `CROSSROADS_KIT.md` as context; do not regenerate its sources during preproduction.

**Composition:** organize receiving and dirty repair near the shutter, small-parts work at the bench, and storage against the wall. The central bay stays open. Yard pipe stacks and pallet storage should make functional sense without narrowing the door approach. The generator remains an existing gameplay object; this art pass does not move or change it.

**Identity:** different-age repairs, sorted useful spares, a maintenance sheet left incomplete, a cloth and mug placed where a worker paused. Keep deterioration concentrated below roof leaks and around handled edges. Do not blanket the room with identical rust.

**Reuse decision:** keep all existing major props. The sourced tool cart is optional, not a shopping requirement; its role must differ from the toolbox and shelf. Small paper records and location-specific markings are original-art gaps after the setting is settled.

**Concepts made:** [dusk exterior](crossroads-dusk-v01.png) and [interior lighting/dressing](crossroads-interior-v01.png), both based on the actual source renders. See [review notes](CONCEPT_REVIEW.md) for deviations and limits. Next unresolved view: yard circulation/storytelling at player eye height. Existing exterior render already supplies a construction reference; no new building redesign is needed.

## B03 — checkpoint: people were processed here

**Keep:** the current hut, barriers, fence, filing cabinet, cot, medical supplies, equipment case and desk radio. Use the sourced office desk only if the current desk solution is insufficient.

**Composition:** entry/inspection surface, administrative desk, records storage, then staff rest and medical supplies. Place wear at the visitor-facing edge and on repeated routes. Containers belong behind the working surface, not randomly across the floor.

**Identity:** reusable inspection tray, key board, numbered records and a roster. Paperwork must communicate procedure before military spectacle. Most portable supplies remain familiar inventory silhouettes. A half-cleared inspection surface suggests interruption without a corpse display.

**Research gaps:** tray/key-board source; period/language-specific documents and insignia. Do not mistake a generic bunker pack for a grounded checkpoint. Next package: one room view and an administrative object board.

## B04 — research: a field operation adapting an ordinary building

**Keep:** existing destination and sample objective, equipment cases, shelves, local lights and utility furniture. The building's current footprint remains authoritative.

**Composition proposal:** receiving surface for sealed cases, a distinct sample-examination bench, clean supply storage, and records/dispatch. This is narrative staging, not a real laboratory procedure or certification of facility design. Keep drinking/eating props away from sample work.

**Identity:** a small, selected group from the Chemistry Set, one Industrial Microscope, repeated transport cases with consistent sample identifiers, visible separation of unused and handled containers. A missing case position can echo the existing retrieval objective. Avoid giant sci-fi reactors and rows of meaningless illuminated screens.

**Reuse decision:** sourced glassware and microscope are the first choices; existing cases/shelving stay. Do not create custom generic beakers. Original art should focus on Ashfall's sample labels, research sketches and anomaly residue after the anomaly direction is chosen.

**Research gaps:** sink/wash surfaces, PPE storage contents, sample transport inserts and task-specific reference photographs. No final interior concept claims a complete equipment list yet. Next package: examination-bench study and source-mapped prop board before a complete room.

## B05 — North Relay: listening and keeping a connection alive

**Keep:** existing yard, boundary/link, recording objective, desk radio, electrical cabinets, cable spool and equipment cases. Relay identity comes from equipment relationships; a new radio tower model is not required to begin.

**Composition proposal:** operator/recording desk, cable termination/service wall and spare-component storage. Cable runs have destinations and remain clear of the normal walking route. Records stay near the operator, tools near servicing.

**Identity:** the recorder beside a log, one test meter at an open service position, organised cables and a reserve component case. Reuse shared furniture but omit the cottage's domestic clusters and research glassware.

**Reuse decision:** Cassette Player is the first recording-device lead; Retro Multimeter is the servicing lead. Keep the local desk radio by default. The military Vintage Radio Transceiver is an alternative only if its size, era and role are justified; never use it to replace the handheld receiver at a wildly different scale.

**Research gaps:** a coherent rack/patch-panel source, cable connectors, recorder controls and period match. Next package: operator-desk concept, service-wall board and a player-height yard view tied to existing geometry.

## Later places

A store, clinic and farm outbuilding are research topics, not approved expansions. Define their place in the journey before sourcing whole packs. A clinic needs treatment furniture rather than a random research bench; a farm needs agricultural storage rather than generic military crates. Each later area follows these same reuse records and concept gates.

## Completion standard before 3D resumes

For each selected building: source-linked object list, editable-source availability, material palette, exterior/interior view, small-detail board, and an annotated layout checked against existing dimensions. Identify every speculative element. Review the concept package as a whole before importing or modifying production scenes. No layout image can substitute for collision, visibility or pursuit tests later.
